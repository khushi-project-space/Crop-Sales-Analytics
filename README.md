# 🌱 Agri Power BI: Crop Sales Analytics Dashboard

A comprehensive data analytics and visualization solution built using **Microsoft Power BI** to track, analyze, and optimize agricultural crop sales, revenue distribution, village-wise performance, and farmer contributions.

## 📊 Project Overview

This dashboard transforms raw agricultural sales data into actionable business intelligence. It provides deep visibility into financial metrics, supply chain volumes, and operational performance across different villages, crops, and payment methods.

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* **Total Revenue:** ₹1,424,247,3... (Over ₹1.42 Billion total transaction value)
* **Total Orders:** 600
* **Total Quantity Sold:** 1,502,178 kg (~1.5 Million kg)
* **Average Order Value:** ₹23.74 Lakh

## 🔍 Key Insights & Visualizations

1. **Village-wise Sales:** Highlights top-performing regions. Leading villages like *Karamsad*, *Bakrol*, and *Borsad* generate the highest revenues (around ₹0.18bn each).
2. **Count of OrderID by Payment Mode:** Analyzes customer payment preferences across **Cash** (160), **Bank Transfer** (155), **UPI** (149), and **Card** (136).
3. **Revenue by Delivery Status:** Tracks fulfillment pipelines broken down into Delivered, Pending, and Cancelled statuses.
4. **Crop-wise Sales (Revenue):** Evaluates demand and revenue shares across various crops such as *Potato*, *Groundnut*, *Maize*, *Cotton*, and *Rice*.
5. **Monthly Sales Trends:** Illustrates seasonality and peak months for agricultural trade (showing strong performance in early spring and mid-year periods).
6. **Top Farmers by Revenue:** Leaderboard showcasing top-earning farmers, led by **Ramesh Patel** (₹18.39 Crore) and **Jayesh Rabari** (₹18.07 Crore).

## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Prerequisites:** Ensure you have [Microsoft Power BI Desktop](https://powerbi.microsoft.com/) installed on your system.
2. **Clone/Download:** Download the repository files (`Project1.pbix` and `Agri_PowerBI_Dataset.xlsx`) to your local machine.
3. **Open Report:** Double-click `Project1.pbix` to launch the dashboard in Power BI.
4. **Interact:** Use the global slicers at the top (**Year**, **Month**, **State**, **Crops**, and **Payment Method**) to dynamically filter and explore regional and crop-specific insights.
